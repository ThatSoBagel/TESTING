Not a network.
A real room.

CCC started as three friends sharing unfinished work at a kitchen table. Now it is a growing, open-minded group of designers, writers, makers, musicians, and people who are still figuring it out. No gatekeeping. No pitch decks. Just good energy and better questions.

Our code

Keep it human. Keep it moving.

01

Stay curious.

Ask the next question before reaching for the answer.

02

Make it generous.

Leave every room with more encouragement than you found.

03

Start small.

There is no perfect time to begin. Begin anyway.
